
## Add a movie (module lists)
- File: `knowledge/lists/movies.md`.
- First: search for the title in both tables (ignore accents and case). If it's there, don't duplicate; say so.
- To watch: row at the end of **To watch** with `Status` = `To watch`. Unknown fields stay empty; don't invent.
- Watched with a rating: move it to **Watched** (`N` = next number) and remove it from To watch. Never change existing ratings or notes.

## Add a series or a book (module lists)
- Files: `knowledge/lists/series.md` and `knowledge/lists/books.md`. Same rules as movies. `Status`: `To start`, `In progress`, `Finished`, `Dropped`.
